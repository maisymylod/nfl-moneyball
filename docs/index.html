Fantasy draft value

Half-PPR redraft, 12-team leagues. ADP from Fantasy Football Calculator. Realized is each player's half-PPR fantasy points in the season the ADP was drawn for. Expected is a robust log-linear fit of realized points vs ADP across the cohort. Surplus = realized minus expected: positive means the pick out-earned its ADP, negative means it under-earned. Players with no recorded stats (missed the year, never played) keep NaN surplus rather than being imputed to zero.
